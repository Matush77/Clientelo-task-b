## Research questions
1. **Ad platform policies** for financial products & services when targeting the Czech Republic / EU:
   - Meta (Facebook/Instagram): financial products and services policy, special ad categories and their targeting restrictions in the EU, advertiser verification, prohibited claims.
   - Google Ads: Financial services verification — is it required for Czechia? What content triggers it?
   - TikTok: financial services ad policy for CZ.
2. **Meta Instant Forms vs. an external landing page** — only facts that matter for us (the assignment requires a landing page).
3. **Regulation of marketing communications for investment products** (Czech/EU): MiFID II Art. 24(3) and Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/565 Art. 44 (fair, clear, not misleading; rules on past performance — e.g. 5-year periods, currency warnings, simulated performance), ESMA guidelines on marketing communications (under the cross-border distribution regulation), ESMA/ČNB statements on finfluencers (2023–2025), ČNB stance. Key question: would a lead-magnet page that compares ETFs (published by a non-broker) count as "investment advice" or an "investment recommendation" (MAR), and what wording keeps it informational? Does a non-licensed publisher need any licence or registration? Present facts and options, no recommendation.
4. **Disclaimers in Czech:** risk warning, past performance, currency risk, "not investment advice" — find real examples from Czech sites and quote short fragments (<15 words).
5. **GDPR for lead forms:** legal basis (performance of a request vs. consent; marketing consent separate), Czech ÚOOÚ guidance, required privacy information, double opt-in practice, consent wording examples.
6. **Czech cookie law:** Act 127/2005 Coll. as amended effective 1 Jan 2022 (opt-in). Impact on analytics and ad pixels: which tools/approaches may run without consent (cookieless analytics, server-side events, Google Consent Mode v2), what must wait for consent. Practical effect on measuring the funnel.
7. **Competitor ads:** use the Meta Ad Library (https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) for Portu, Fondee, Finax, XTB, Trading 212 and Czech finance educators. Record active ad angles, hooks, formats, CTAs, and which landing pages they point to. The Ad Library is JS-heavy — if you cannot access it via WebFetch, state that clearly and use other evidence (press about their campaigns, marketing case studies, Google Ads Transparency Center). Never fabricate ad content.
8. **Ad creative and copy patterns** that perform for finance on Meta/TikTok (hooks, UGC vs. static, carousels, numbers in headlines, compliance-safe wording) — with evidence strength (Strong / Medium / Weak).
