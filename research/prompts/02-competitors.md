## For each of the 8 sites
- URL of the specific page analysed (prefer pages that receive ad traffic, e.g. dedicated campaign landing pages or pages linked from their ads, if findable).
- **First screen on mobile:** headline, subheadline, main CTA text, what is above the fold. (You read HTML/text, not the rendering — say when you are inferring layout.)
- **Value offered in exchange for the contact** (lead magnet) and whether the value is shown before the form.
- **Moment of the ask:** where/when the contact form appears (immediately, after an interaction, after scrolling, popup, exit-intent, gated result).
- **Form:** number of fields, which fields, consent checkboxes, double opt-in hints.
- **Interactive elements** (calculators, comparisons, quizzes, portfolio builders) and how each leads to the form.
- **Trust signals** (regulation/licences, client numbers, reviews, media logos, team faces).
- **Disclaimers and risk warnings.**
- **What is promised after submission.**
- **Tone and visual style** (formal/informal, "ty" vs. "vy" addressing, emoji, colours, imagery).
- **Notable weaknesses.**
