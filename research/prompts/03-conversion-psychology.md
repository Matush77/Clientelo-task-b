# Agent C — Conversion & psychology evidence

Output file: `C:\Users\matus\Desktop\B_project\research\03-conversion-psychology.md`

Goal: evidence on what turns mobile visitors from paid social ads into leads, ideally for finance/investing.

Rate the evidence strength of every claim:
- **Strong** = peer-reviewed study, large-sample experiment, or multi-company dataset.
- **Medium** = credible industry dataset or a documented A/B test with numbers.
- **Weak** = opinion or vendor blog without data.

## Research questions
1. **Lead-magnet types** (calculator, quiz, comparison tool, report/e-book, checklist, email course, personalised result) — what evidence exists on their conversion and on lead quality?
2. **Gated vs. "show value first, gate the full/personalised result"** — evidence on when to ask for the contact (upfront vs. after interaction), including foot-in-the-door / commitment-consistency, sunk effort, IKEA effect, endowment with personalised results.
3. **Forms:** number of fields, single- vs. multi-step forms, inline vs. popup/modal, email-only vs. name+email, phone fields, effect of consent checkboxes (any GDPR-era evidence), button copy.
4. **Message match** between ad and landing page — evidence on effect.
5. **First screen / 5-second principles on mobile:** what must be above the fold, headline clarity, visual hierarchy, sticky CTA, thumb zone, page weight and load time effects on conversion (e.g. Google/Deloitte "Milliseconds make millions", web.dev case studies).
6. **Trust in finance:** effect of data sources, regulatory statements, social proof, faces, reviews, security badges on conversion.
7. **Relevant biases** for this context with evidence: loss aversion and fee framing (e.g. showing fees as CZK lost over 20 years vs. % TER), anchoring, default effects, scarcity/urgency (and their risks in finance), choice overload when comparing many funds.
8. **Post-submit experience:** instant delivery vs. delayed, thank-you page as a second conversion step, welcome email open rates, double opt-in effect on list quality.
9. **Benchmarks:** median landing-page conversion rate for finance / financial services (e.g. Unbounce Conversion Benchmark Report), lead conversion from Meta/TikTok traffic in finance, cost per lead ranges in CZ/CEE if available, email open/click rates for finance newsletters. Give ranges and sample sizes; note the year.
10. **Czech copy considerations:** any evidence on "tykání" vs. "vykání" in Czech marketing (especially finance) and on Czech consumers' response to aggressive vs. sober sales tone.

Where to look: Unbounce, CXL, Baymard Institute, Nielsen Norman Group, VWO / Optimizely / GoodUI case libraries, HubSpot research, web.dev case studies, Google research, academic literature (Google Scholar: interactive content, calculators, personalised feedback, loss framing of fees, foot-in-the-door online). Document A/B tests with their reported numbers and sample sizes when available.
