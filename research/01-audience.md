# Audience: the Czech retail ETF investor

Research agent A (Claude Sonnet 5.5). All sources accessed 2026-10-10. Tags: `[VERIFIED]` = page or file opened and states it; `[INFERRED]` = my reasoning, or only a search snippet / headline / secondary summary was available.

Method notes: Reddit, Facebook groups and YouTube comments could not be accessed (Reddit is blocked for the fetch tool and search crawler; the others are not fetchable). The web search budget ran out mid-task, so some gaps stay open. Voice-of-customer therefore comes mainly from the FinExpert.cz (e15) public forum and Czech App Store reviews. Quotes were returned through the fetch tool's text-extraction layer, so check spelling against the original before using any of them in copy.

## Summary

- Surveys disagree on "how many Czechs invest" because definitions differ: 39% (Raiffeisenbank/NMS, Feb 2026), 41% (AKAT, Feb 2025), 55% (Česká spořitelna/Ipsos, Mar 2026), 57% "have experience" (Generali/Ipsos, Apr 2025). The only ETF-specific figure found is "about 14%" (Generali, 2025). *(Lead-agent check: the source says ETFs "hold a stable share of around 14%" without saying of what. It most likely means a share among investors' products, **not** 14% of all Czechs. Do not use it as a population figure. See 99-verification-log A3.)* No official count of Czech ETF holders exists in what I found.
- Typical investor profile across surveys: male, younger, higher income, university-educated. Women are about 38–40% of Portu clients and the fastest-growing group there. No regional data found.
- Top motivation is retirement (50% to 78% depending on survey). Inflation protection is a secondary driver (39%). The DIP tax deduction (up to 48,000 CZK a year) is a large new driver: 244,217 DIP contracts on 31 Mar 2026.
- Top barriers among non-investors are fear of losing money (73%) and a feeling of lacking money or know-how (71%) (Raiffeisenbank). Fear of fraud (43%, AKAT 2024) and distrust of brokers also appear.
- Platforms: Česká spořitelna (more than 780k investing clients), Portu (more than 280k clients), Trading 212, XTB, Revolut, Degiro, Fio, Patria. No Czech client counts are published for XTB or Trading 212.
- Czech investors discuss European UCITS ETFs (VWCE, IWDA, SWRD, SPYL, CSPX/SXR8, VUAA), not US tickers. US-domiciled ETFs are unavailable to EU retail clients at most brokers because of PRIIPs (missing local-language documents). Fio and Patria are named exceptions. *(Lead-agent check: the Finex source names only SPY as the example, not VOO or IVV, and says "PRIIPs" without naming the KID. See 99-verification-log A1.)* This matters for a page about NYSE ETFs.
- The questions people get stuck on: S&P 500 vs world index, accumulating vs distributing, TER (0.03% to 0.14% in the sources above), broker choice, CZK/USD conversion cost, and tax (3-year time test, 15% vs 35% dividend withholding).
- Reach: Facebook ad reach 5.0M, Instagram 3.95M, TikTok 2.13M (18+), YouTube 8.08M (DataReportal, Oct 2025). Mobile is only 49.3% of all Czech web traffic (StatCounter, Sep 2026). No Czech data on landing-page behaviour after ad clicks was found.
- Sub-segments with evidence: bank-savings beginners, DIY optimisers, DIP/pension savers 40+, parents saving for children, under-30 social-media-led, women. Each is listed as an option below.

### 2. Motivations

- Retirement reserve 78%, better living standard 63%, protection against inflation 39% (Raiffeisenbank, Jul 2026). [VERIFIED] https://www.e15.cz/finexpert/banky-a-ucty/strach-ze-ztraty-jako-hlavni-brzda-proc-cesi-stale-vahaji-s-investovanim-1434418
- Pension as the main reason: 50% in 2025 vs 43% in 2024 (AKAT). [VERIFIED] https://www.newstream.cz/money/ctyri-z-deseti-cechu-se-povazuji-za-investory
- An economist quoted by Seznam Zprávy calls the inflation shock a "wake-up call". He says the lasting trend is driven by mobile apps and the DIP. [VERIFIED] (Seznam Zprávy link above).
- DIP tax benefit: deduction of up to 48,000 CZK a year from the tax base, and employer contributions up to 50,000 CZK free of social and health insurance. [VERIFIED] https://zpravy.kurzy.cz/856684-s-dip-lide-investuji-na-duchod-jiz-9-5-miliardy-korun-pocet-smluv-se-blizi-ctvrt-milionu/
- Social media: 53% of 16–30 year olds follow finfluencers, 25% have invested on their advice (5% regularly), 21% name influencers as a regular source; 52% turn to family and friends; 78% at least sometimes verify. [VERIFIED] (GSFT link above).
- Portu goals by age: under 29 housing and reserve; 30–49 assets, children, retirement; over 50 income protection. [VERIFIED] (Portu/kurzy link above).
- FIRE: No evidence found in Czech surveys.

### 3. Fears and objections

- Non-investors: fear of loss 73%, lack of money and know-how 71% (Raiffeisenbank 2026). [VERIFIED]
- Česká spořitelna: fear of loss is the top barrier; 46% of non-investors would start if sure not to lose money; about one fifth would be helped by an explanation of how to start. [VERIFIED] (Seznam Zprávy link above).
- AKAT Feb 2024: fear of fraud 43% (+10 points), risk 42%. [VERIFIED] (ČT24 link above).
- Knowledge gap: only a third correctly said an equity fund share is safer than a single share; only half knew buying more shares lowers risk (Mar 2024). [VERIFIED] (soc.cas.cz link above).
- Fees: forum discussion centres on TER differences of hundredths of a percent and on broker, FX and inactivity fees (see voice of customer). [VERIFIED]
- Tax and rule instability: a DIP forum poster doubts the 3-year time test will last (3 Oct 2026). XTB's app reviews complain that Czech dividends are taxed at 35%. [VERIFIED] XTB's own review page lists 35% on Czech dividends and 15% on US dividends with W-8BEN. [VERIFIED] https://finex.cz/recenze/xtb/
- Currency risk (CZK vs USD): raised repeatedly by beginners, along with the cost of hedging. [VERIFIED, 2021 thread, outdated] https://forum.finexpert.e15.cz/viewtopic.php?f=312&t=1317960
- Distrust of brokers: Sep 2026 forum thread on broker diversification, with posters trusting Interactive Brokers and Saxo and doubting cheaper brokers. [VERIFIED] https://forum.finexpert.e15.cz/viewtopic.php?f=312&t=1343626
- Czech authority warnings on scams (fake ČNB licences, Mar 2026). [INFERRED, search snippet only]

### 5. Comparison triggers and stuck decisions

- **US vs UCITS availability.** Finex (updated 25 Sep 2026) says the largest US ETFs are unavailable to EU retail clients at most brokers because of PRIIPs and missing local-language documents. Fio (SPY, 7.95 USD per order) and Patria are named exceptions. [VERIFIED] https://finex.cz/nejzajimavejsi-etf-fondy-kopirujici-sp-500/ Portu's magazine said the same in 2019 (missing KIID). [VERIFIED, outdated] https://magazin.portu.cz/etf-na-sp-500-vybrat-spy-nebo-jine/ A forum search (Jan 2025 posts) shows some brokers block ETFs without a Czech KID. [VERIFIED]
- **S&P 500 vs world.** A Sep 2026 forum reply says "whoever does not want pure S&P 500 should take the new VGLA". Another poster says comparing S&P 500 with a world index is "apples-to-oranges". [VERIFIED]
- **Accumulating vs distributing.** Reasons given: tax (no dividend to declare after the 3-year test), simplicity. [VERIFIED] https://finex.cz/nejzajimavejsi-etf-fondy-kopirujici-sp-500/
- **TER hunting.** SPYL 0.03%, CSPX/SXR8 0.07%, VWCE listed at 0.14% by a broker-comparison site (a Sep 2026 forum post says it was recently cut). The Invesco MSCI World ETF (SC0J) TER cut to 0.05% on 31 Mar 2026 triggered discussion. [VERIFIED/INFERRED mix; forum thread https://forum.finexpert.e15.cz/viewtopic.php?f=312&t=1342864]
- **Same index, different price.** 2021 thread: "why do two S&P 500 funds have different prices?" (ticker, currency, venue). [VERIFIED, 2021] https://forum.finexpert.e15.cz/viewtopic.php?f=312&t=1320610
- **Broker choice and costs.** Free-ETF lists (Degiro), FX conversion (0.5% at XTB), inactivity fees (Interactive Brokers), moving money in and out. [VERIFIED]
- **Moments of triggering.** Employer DIP contribution (Nov 2025 thread), tax-return season, new fund launches (VGLA, Sep 2026), market falls and inflation. [VERIFIED/INFERRED]
- **Search-volume data** ("jaké ETF koupit", Google Trends): No evidence found (not accessible).

## Contradictions & open questions

- "How many invest" ranges from 39% to 57%. Each survey uses a different definition (investing vs "let money work" vs "experience"), sampling and age bracket. HFCS 2020 shows only 7% of households with mutual funds, which predates the boom and uses a different base. No source gives a national share holding ETFs.
- ETF share: Generali says about 14% and "stable" among Czechs, while XTB claims ETFs are 42% of its own clients' investment forms. These measure different populations.
- Mobile-first assumption: only 49.3% of all Czech web traffic is mobile. Paid-social traffic is likely higher, but I found no Czech figure. [INFERRED]
- Benchmarks conflict on whether mobile or desktop converts better for finance (Unbounce vs CuFinder).
- US ETF availability: Finex (2026) says only Fio and Patria offer SPY-type funds; Patria says 72% of retail trades are on US markets (stocks and ETFs not separated). Whether Czech retail can buy NYSE-listed ETFs in practice depends on broker and ETF, and is outside this agent's scope.
- Tax rules: secondary sources disagree on whether the 100,000 CZK value test and time-test limits changed from 2026 (search snippets only). Forum posters fear the 3-year test may be repealed. Not verified here; check with a legal agent.
- Portu iOS rating count (14) does not match its client base. Fondee and Revolut Czech client numbers are old or undated.
- A zero in forum search for 3-letter tickers is an artefact (the search engine ignores them), not evidence of no demand for VOO, SPY or QQQ.
- Not found: Reddit, Facebook and YouTube voice of customer; regional data; Czech ad-click device split; Czech search volumes; Czech behaviour on ad landing pages; official ČNB count of ETF investors.
- The Česká spořitelna Index prosperity release was behind a 403, so the sample size is from a snippet. Several PDFs were read through pdftotext (encoding mangled, numbers legible).

## Options & trade-offs (input for later decisions, no recommendation)

| Option | Pro | Con |
|---|---|---|
| Target beginners with bank savings (A) | Largest pool, strong fear and "how to start" gap, ad reach large | Low intent, long path, trust barrier; NYSE-listed ETFs are mostly not buyable by them |
| Target DIY optimisers (B) | Active questions (TER, venue, acc/dist, FX, tax), existing UCITS vocabulary, forum evidence of repeat comparison | Smaller, sceptical of marketing, many free tools exist |
| Target DIP / 40+ savers (C) | Clear tax hook (48,000 CZK), growing fast (244k), retirement is the strongest motive | Provider-specific, regulation of retirement claims, older audience may be less mobile-only |
| Parents (D) / women (F) / under-30 (E) | Distinct value propositions; fast growth at Portu | Less quantitative evidence on size; E needs TikTok/Instagram and is distrustful |
| Frame "NYSE ETFs" as US-index reference and map to UCITS equivalents (SPYL, CSPX, VUAA) | Matches vocabulary and availability seen in Czech sources | Departs from literal NYSE-listed tickers (SPY, VOO) that most EU retail cannot buy |
| Frame around the few brokers that sell US ETFs (Fio, Patria) | Literal NYSE products | Narrow audience, broker partnership or compliance questions |
| Lead magnet types the evidence points to | Fee comparison (TER and FX), tax guide (time test, dividend withholding), "which ETF" checklist | Must be verified against current tax law and cannot be personalised advice |
