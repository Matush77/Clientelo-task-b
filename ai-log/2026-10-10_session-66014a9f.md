v6 is ready to review ([design/previews/v6.html](design/previews/v6.html), commit `16c3e89`). The outlined phone is a working prototype: menu, calculator, form and breakdown.

**What changed from v5**
- **Background:** lifted from pure black to a soft dark grey (`#18191c`).
- **Hero:** "Stejný index. Jiná cena.", "Zjistěte, kolik vám ETF ukusuje na poplatcích." (no "10 vteřin"), then the three checks and the CTA.
- **Website feel:** a sticky header with the logo, a "Jak počítáme" link and a menu icon that opens a full-screen menu. A short "Jak počítáme" section with the sources and a footer with links and the legal disclaimer sit below the calculator.
- **Result chart:** two horizontal cost bars with values, replacing the vertical blocks.
- **Spacing:** one consistent spacing scale, more air between sections and inside cards.
- **Phone is opt-in:** only name and e-mail are required. The "Chci zavolat" switch reveals the phone field and makes it required only while it's on. The thank-you text changes to match ("Zavoláme vám jednou…"). This is also cleaner legally, because it gives explicit consent for that one call.
- **CTA A/B:** the toggle at the top of the page switches every button between "Začít investovat" and "Spočítat poplatky". It's recorded as an A/B hypothesis in [research/decisions.md](research/decisions.md).

**Open items before the build**
- **Numbers are illustrative.** They assume monthly purchases on both routes, which makes Fio's USD 7.95 commission look very heavy at small amounts. For a fair comparison (MiFID Art. 44 sets "fair and balanced" as the standard), the real calculator should probably batch the Fio orders, e.g. quarterly. That's a build-phase choice I'll put in the plan.
- **Index coverage:** only S&P 500 is wired up. "Celý svět" and "Celý trh USA" need verified fund fee (TER) data for VT and VTI first.

If v6 looks right, the next step is the build plan: stack, hosting, form backend, e-mail delivery and tracking. If not, tell me what to change.
