Minimal fintech: 5 directions (v2)

Rebuilt after feedback: one statement + one action per screen, built for a 2-second decision. Flow = 1 tap → result → form sheet with autofill. Same copy in all five; only the visual language changes. The number is an illustrative scenario (3 000 Kč/month + 100 000 Kč, 20 years) from the verified fee data; brand "Dvojče" is a placeholder.

01Noc: dark, one neon accent

Robinhood / Cash App feel. Maximum contrast; the number glows. Feels like an app, not a website.

02Svěží: light, fresh green

Wise-like friendliness: bright, rounded, heavy type. Green reads as "money saved" without being a bank.

03Čistá: white + one cobalt

Mercury / Stripe calm. Lightest weight, most whitespace; the blue number is the only colour on screen.

04Barva: full-bleed colour field

N26 / Monzo boldness: deep teal background, warm coral action. Most thumb-stopping right after the ad.

05Teplá: warm neutral, soft cards

Apple Card / Revolut-light softness: warm off-white, floating white cards, black buttons, one orange highlight.
