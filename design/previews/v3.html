Veenos: dark minimal (v3)

Direction 01 "Noc" chosen; the neon lime felt too loud. Part A tests five calmer accents on the arrival screen. Part B shows the full flow in that design: 3 one-tap steps → result → form sheet → full breakdown. Use the toggle to recolour the whole flow. Numbers: illustrative scenario (100 000 Kč + 3 000 Kč/month, 20 years, 7 % p.a. before costs) from the verified fee data.

A · Accent

Same screen, five accents on the same near-black. The accent appears only on: the logo dot, the key word, the selected chip and the main button.

B · Full flow

Each screen = one idea, one action. Three taps to the number, three fields (autofill) to the breakdown.
