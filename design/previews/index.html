Five visual directions, same content

Screen 1 = what a visitor sees on arrival from the ad ("same index, cheaper twin" angle). Screen 2 = after "Porovnat": headline result visible, details locked behind the 3-field form. Copy, numbers and structure are identical across directions; only the visual language changes. Numbers are an illustrative scenario (100 000 Kč + 3 000 Kč/month, 20 years, 7 % p.a. before costs; Fio FX spread ≈ 3 % indicative), computed from the verified fee data. Brand name "ETF dvojčata" is a placeholder.

01

Deník — data journalism

Reads like a trusted financial newspaper: serif headlines, tabular figures, thin rules, numbered source footnotes. Credibility through sources, not decoration.

Fits a sceptical DIY investor who distrusts ads (73 % say ads exaggerate).

Risk: may feel slow or editorial for a 3-second ad visit; needs a strong first-screen CTA.

Fee salience in CZK is the best-evidenced lever in the research.

Risk: the metaphor carries the page; the first screen must still look credible, not gimmicky.

Mřížka — Swiss grid

Risk: can feel cold; relies entirely on typography being done well.
